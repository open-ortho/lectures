## Event announcement

**Title:** AI and Reusable Clinical Data

**Course:** Orthodontic Informatics · Module 3

A lecture for second-year orthodontic residents on making clinical data reusable by authorized AI tools. The session explores how normalized, structured data and interconnected systems can reduce repeated manual collection and reformatting, while requiring deliberate decisions about trust and access. DICOM, HL7 FHIR, and IHE profiles illustrate how existing standards support clinical exchange; a proposed clinical workflow shows why connections alone do not guarantee safe or accurate AI.

**Speaker:** Toni Magni, Clinical Instructor, Department of Orthodontics,
School of Dental Medicine, Case Western Reserve University; afm@case.edu.

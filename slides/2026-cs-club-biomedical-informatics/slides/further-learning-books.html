Further learning: books

Optional follow-up—not prerequisites for this talk.

Hacking Healthcare
Fred Trotter & David Uhlman · O’Reilly, 2011
Clinical workflows and IT context. US-focused and dated; not current policy guidance.

Health Informatics on FHIR
Mark L. Braunstein · Springer, 2nd ed., 2022
Build interoperable apps; explore the API ecosystem.
Mixed clinical and CS/IT audience; not the whole field.

  • Workflow context: Trotter and Uhlman bridge IT and clinical work; their 2011 US policy discussion is historical.
  • App ecosystem: Braunstein connects FHIR APIs to healthcare use cases for mixed clinical and CS/IT readers, not every informatics area.

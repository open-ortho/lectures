56–60 · Synthesis and M4

  • Export demonstration: have another product interpret the case, rather than merely show that a file can be downloaded.
  • Permitted retrieval: demonstrate which patient/visit links, dates, units and source references survive under the intended account limits.
  • Inspectable evidence: show original sources, missing information and rejected unauthorized requests—not only a successful answer.
  • Reviewed result: show where it is saved, with which patient/visit links, and which identity has separately authorized permission to save it.
  • Module 4: apply these questions to product demonstrations, contracts and archive control—who can retain and reuse records if software changes.
  • Open-Ortho: contribute clinical requirements to the conversation — https://www.open-ortho.org/ ; no vendor selection or patient-data upload is authorized here.
  • Closing prompt: “I would not accept the demonstration unless…” Reusable information still needs clinical interpretation; finish within the scheduled hour.

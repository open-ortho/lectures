56–60 · Synthesis and M4

Close by giving residents an action, not a shopping verdict: bring these requirements into product demonstrations and local evaluation. M4 will examine procurement, export/import, contracts, archive control and red flags. We have not selected a vendor, run a clinical integration or authorized patient-data upload. The transfer is from a useful clinical need to a testable requirement: name the actor, operation, expected record context and observable failure behavior. Clinicians can contribute those requirements to vendors and standards communities, including through Open-Ortho. Invite one final resident to complete the sentence I would not accept the demonstration unless… and connect the answer to one of the four questions. End at sixty minutes, with the distinction between reusable information and correct clinical interpretation intact.

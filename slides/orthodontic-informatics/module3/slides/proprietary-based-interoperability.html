New partner: another bridge—or reuse the same implementation?

27–39 · Choose an exchange contract

  • Implementation: actual exchange software—not just documentation or a standard's name.
  • Vendor pair: a custom bridge translates between two products' APIs and data definitions; another partner needs another pair-specific integration.
  • Three partners: A–B, A–C and A–D mean three bridges to build, test and maintain in this example.
  • Implement once, reuse: A's standards implementation serves compatible B, C and D without a new translator for each pair; each partner implements its side.
  • Compatibility: agree the standard, version, profile and operations; shared standards do not remove all configuration or testing.
  • Central difference: partner-specific integration software versus reusable standards software—not merely private versus published documentation.
  • Scope: APIs and adapters can exist in both approaches; judge actual reuse and retained clinical context.

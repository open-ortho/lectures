27–39 · Choose an exchange contract

Compare equivalent integration approaches: integration using vendor-specific interfaces versus integration using shared open clinical standards. Do not compare an API with a standard as though only one side has an interface; either approach may use APIs and either may need mapping software. A vendor-specific interface can be well documented, versioned and maintained. A shared standard offers common definitions and reusable tools but still leaves choices to implementations. In our case the test is the same: can the receiving product interpret the permitted records while retaining identity, timing, units and source links? Clinical products may use both approaches in one workflow. Invite residents to identify a question that applies to both, such as which records are supported or what happens after an upgrade. Avoid declaring either approach inherently safe or clinically accurate.

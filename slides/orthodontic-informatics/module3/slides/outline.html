Which records would you trust it to use—and why?

Introduce T-017 as a wholly synthetic case used throughout, not a real patient or a deployed integration. Invite a resident to name one record needed for the brief and one context item needed to interpret it. An image needs a date and identity; a measurement needs its method and units; a historical note needs its relationship to the visit. Avoid drawing a diagnosis from these invented data. The intended output separates what the record says from what the tool infers and from information it cannot find. Ask whether the brief would be useful if it omitted a recent visit. This reveals why fluent prose is not sufficient evidence that the right records arrived. We will keep the requested output constant while changing how information reaches the tool.

7–15 · Who is adapting?

  • Mailbox data: MBOX is a mailbox file format holding messages; the envelope/store supplies data to the mail service.
  • IMAP: Internet Message Access Protocol lets clients access and manage messages and folders through that service; no sending transport is shown.
  • Mail client: the interface a person uses—Webmail (GMail, ...), or a mail app such as Apple Mail or Thunderbird.
  • Requests: the client asks the service for operations and receives results; the human's two-way interaction is with the client.
  • Mailbox authentication: at the dashed line, the client proves the human account's identity using configured credentials, such as a token.
  • Permissions: the service enforces what that account may read or change; the line highlights mailbox authentication, not every security boundary.
  • Conceptual diagram: illustrative, not a claim about Gmail storage or internal webmail protocols; providers need not use MBOX or this exact path.

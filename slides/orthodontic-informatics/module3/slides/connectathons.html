39–49 · Shape and test exchange

  • Connectathon: structured, supervised exchange testing between participating implementations—not a solo product demonstration.
  • Selected roles: IHE (Integrating the Healthcare Enterprise) calls system roles “actors”; each test covers specified responsibilities, not the entire product.
  • Defined use cases: profiles specify workflows and transactions—the required exchanges of messages or records—against which implementations are tested.
  • Published results: ask what implementation, role and profile actually tested successfully; participation or a logo alone does not establish a pass.
  • Photograph: historical IHE North America testing setting, not evidence that these participants tested P-18, our tools or any AI system.
  • Local question: which parts of our clinical requirement do those results support, and which still need testing in our clinic?

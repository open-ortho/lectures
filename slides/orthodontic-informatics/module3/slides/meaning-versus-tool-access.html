27–39 · Choose an exchange contract

  • Clinical exchange contract: agreed record structure and meaning; DICOM supplies imaging rules and applicable HL7 standards cover broader clinical exchange.
  • Profiles and terminology: constraints narrow choices; agreed codes define meanings. Both help sender and receiver interpret the same record.
  • AI tool access: the harness (software managing model/tool interaction) executes selected operations; MCP (Model Context Protocol) can expose those tools consistently.
  • Different jobs: requesting a document successfully does not establish that its diagnosis, visit link or measurement is clinically meaningful.
  • Resource service: the system holding the records enforces what the requesting identity may read or change; available tools do not grant permission.
  • Failure example: a tool returns “6” but drops its unit: the request succeeded, yet the exchange lost meaning needed for the clinical task.

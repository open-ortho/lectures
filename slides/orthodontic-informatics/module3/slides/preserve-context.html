15–27 · Make context reusable

Move the record, not just the words

QuestionContext that travels
Whose record? Which visit?Patient + encounter identifiers and their source
When? What was measured?Dates, time context, value, units + method
Where did this come from?Original note/image + source links
What changed in transit?Transformation history + uncertainty

Provenance = where information came from and how it was transformed.

Walk through a single measurement rather than reading the table. Six is not meaningful without knowing what was measured, its units, its method, the patient and when it was observed. Patient identifiers may be local to an organization; identical-looking strings do not establish a cross-system match. Encounter identity connects information to the relevant clinical visit, while image acquisition and note-entry dates can differ. Preserve those distinctions. Provenance links a derived value or summary to the original record and any conversion or interpretation applied. If a match or date is uncertain, the receiving system should surface it for review rather than silently choosing. Original notes and images remain available: a normalized value is not a replacement for them. Ask which row a screenshot alone is least likely to preserve in machine-usable form.

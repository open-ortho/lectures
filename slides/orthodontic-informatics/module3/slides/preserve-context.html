15–27 · Make context reusable

  • Patient and encounter: an encounter is a visit; retain both identifiers and who issued them. Identical local IDs need not identify the same patient.
  • Dates and time context: image acquisition, measurement and note-entry times can differ; preserve which event each date describes.
  • Value, units and method: “6” needs the measured feature, its unit and how it was measured before another tool can interpret it.
  • Originals and source links: retain the note/image and a traceable reference so the clinician can check a derived value or summary.
  • Transformation history: record conversions or interpretations, such as a date-format change, rather than hiding what changed in transit.
  • Uncertainty: surface ambiguous patient/visit matches or dates for review instead of silently choosing one.
  • Provenance: the chain from source through transformations to the result; normalized fields supplement, not replace, the original record.

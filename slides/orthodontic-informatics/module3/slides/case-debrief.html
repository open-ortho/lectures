49–56 · Consolidate the argument

  • DICOM: Digital Imaging and Communications in Medicine covers images plus related metadata, such as patient/study identifiers, and services such as image storage and retrieval—not just pixels.
  • HL7 FHIR: Health Level Seven's Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources represents structured records such as Patient, Encounter (visit), Observation (measurement) and DocumentReference (document link).
  • Beyond images: FHIR supports wider clinical/administrative context; it can also describe imaging studies and reference images. DICOM and FHIR are complementary, not mutually exclusive containers.
  • IHE profiles: Integrating the Healthcare Enterprise specifies how systems use established standards, such as DICOM and HL7, together for a defined workflow; profiles are not a universal replacement standard.

7–15 · Who is adapting?

In the presenter's workflow the MCP servers are filtered or modified, and agents have their own accounts with independently restricted permissions on the underlying resources. Removing an operation from a tool list narrows what the harness can request through that route. It is not proof that the same account cannot reach the resource through another route. Resource-side permissions therefore matter independently: the service checks what this particular identity may read or change. Conversely, a restricted account may still expose more operations than this task needs, so tool filtering is also useful. Do not turn this into a security guarantee or deployment tutorial. Ask which layer should reject a forbidden resource request even if a model asks convincingly: the service enforcing the account's permissions, not the model's discretion.

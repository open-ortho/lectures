Clinicians define the requirement

“Prepare a visit brief” → a requirement teams can test

The receiving tool must show each measurement with the patient, visit, date, units and source—and flag an uncertain match.

You supply the clinical meaning and failure conditions.
Developers implement the exchange.

Ask what harm could follow from a measurement attached to the wrong encounter or a current diagnosis inferred from an old note. Residents know enough clinical workflow to identify these requirements even without writing an API. A clinical requirement states the actor, requested action and observable output: the receiving tool presents a source-linked measurement for the correct case and marks an uncertain match. This does not require residents to choose a wire format. Clinicians work with standards experts, developers and information-governance teams to describe use cases, missing information and unacceptable failure modes. They are not merely consumers waiting for vendors to decide what a useful record contains. Use the displayed requirement as an example and ask one resident to add a concrete missing-data behavior, such as showing that an expected record was not supplied rather than treating absence as a negative finding.

0–7 · Useful delegation

  • P-18 inputs: same five tasks and same fictional patient; no real patient data or deployed clinical result.
  • Treatment presentation: diagnosis, clinician-approved plan and options give the explanation its clinical content.
  • History capture: compare patient responses with existing history; a missing record is not a negative finding.
  • Consent preparation: use the proposed treatment, risks and alternatives; drafting does not replace the clinician's consent discussion.
  • Photo/scan organization: originals plus patient, date and view distinguish the right record from a similar-looking file.
  • Apps and portals: identify the correct case and required upload set; give limited access, not a shared full-access login.
  • Two separate questions: how will AI obtain the records, and why trust them? Access is not evidence of reliability.
  • Trust checks: inspect source, completeness and currency; flag uncertainty rather than letting availability imply clinical truth.

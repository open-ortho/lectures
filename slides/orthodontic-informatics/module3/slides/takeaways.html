56–60 · Synthesis and M4

  • Reusable case: the next authorized tool should not depend on the clinician collecting, reformatting and explaining the same context again.
  • Context and originals: preserve patient/visit links, dates, units and sources; retain uncertainty rather than replace original records with guesses.
  • Meaning, not just connection: clinical-exchange standards define record meaning; tool protocols expose operations. A successful request can still return an uninterpretable record.
  • Two restrictions: limit exposed tool operations and independently restrict service-side account rights; the model's discretion is not access enforcement.
  • Review and test: clinicians check outputs; exercise the intended exchange and failures such as a missing unit, not just a fluent demonstration.
  • Story boundary: my email workflow is actual experience; the clinical analogy remains proposed, with extra identity, meaning and review requirements.

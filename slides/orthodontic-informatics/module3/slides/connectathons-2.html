39–49 · Shape and test exchange

Invite a resident to interpret a hypothetical claim that a product participated in a Connectathon. Participation alone does not identify a passed test, and even a passed test needs its product, version where available, actor, profile and transactions. Ask the vendor for the actual scope and relevant published results, then compare them with what the clinic intends to do. Event testing is not blanket product certification or local acceptance, and it does not validate an AI interpretation of a record. Local evaluation must check configured versions, patient matching, denied access, record completeness, source fidelity, review and any permitted writeback. Keep the distinction proportional: bounded testing is valuable precisely because its scope can be inspected. Transition by telling residents they will now act as the clinical requirements team for P-18, rather than as passive viewers of a demonstration.

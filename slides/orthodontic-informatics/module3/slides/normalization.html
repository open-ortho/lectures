15–27 · Make context reusable

  • Repeated reconstruction: every new tool infers a visit from filenames and guesses a unit from prose; repeated guesses are not new source evidence.
  • Visit links: store the record's patient/encounter (visit) association so the next authorized tool can reuse it instead of rebuilding it.
  • Values, units and sources: keep the measurement with its stated unit and source reference; an absent unit remains missing, not silently assumed.
  • Unresolved matches: flag competing or missing visit/patient links for review; a confident match is still an inference unless supported.
  • Normalization: make representations consistent—for example, an unambiguous date—without erasing narrative nuance or forcing every observation into a number.
  • Rewriting originals: overwriting an original note, image or measurement with normalized or inferred output; a guessed unit must not replace an ambiguous source.
  • Preserve both: retain the original plus the derived representation, linked to its source and transformation history; distinguish observed, derived and unresolved information.

49–56 · Apply the argument

Run this as four minutes, not a rhetorical question. Give thirty seconds of silent reading, two minutes in pairs, then ninety seconds for two groups to report. The proposal is deliberately flawed and contains no real patient information. Ask each group to name one missing item of context, one unnecessary access right and an acceptance requirement someone can demonstrate. Do not ask them to diagnose the synthetic patient or infer the missing unit. If discussion stalls, ask whether history not supplied means history unremarkable, and whether a draft-preparation tool needs the clinician's full access. Encourage an explicit actor/action/output sentence: when the receiving tool encounters a measurement without units, it flags the omission and links to the original rather than inventing a value. Collect proposals before revealing the next slide so the activity genuinely tests application.

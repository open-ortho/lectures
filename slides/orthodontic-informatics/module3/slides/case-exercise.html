Takeaways: making AI more useful

  • Normalized, structured data can greatly increase AI’s benefits.
  • Interconnected systems can greatly increase AI’s benefits.
  • Connecting systems to AI requires care with trust and access.

49–56 · Consolidate the argument

  • Normalized, structured data: consistent identifiers, dates, units and fields reduce repeated guessing; AI can focus on the clinical task. Keep original notes and images alongside derived values.
  • Interconnected systems: authorized tools can retrieve relevant records and return results without repeated copying, reformatting and re-entry.
  • Greater benefit: better data and connections enable more useful workflows; they do not guarantee a correct model answer or a fixed performance gain.
  • Trust: check where records came from, whether they are complete/current and whether external content or generated output can be relied on; clinician review remains necessary.
  • Access: decide which records AI may read and which actions it may request; enforce limits through tools and service-side account permissions, not model discretion.
  • Separate permissions: reading records or drafting text does not automatically permit sending messages or saving to the chart.

49–56 · Apply the argument

  • Four-minute exercise: synthetic P-18 only; 30 seconds silent reading, two minutes in pairs, 90 seconds for two groups to report.
  • “Overjet 6”: overjet is the horizontal relationship of upper/lower incisors; date and unit are absent—do not diagnose or infer a missing unit.
  • Image match: a P-18 label does not identify the encounter (visit); ask which missing association prevents confident reuse.
  • Missing history: “not supplied” describes the input, not the patient; it cannot support writing “history unremarkable.”
  • Excessive permission: a draft-preparation task does not need the clinician's full-access login; identify the narrower access actually required.
  • Unreviewed saving: a proposed summary is not an approved chart entry; clinician review and permission to save are separate requirements.
  • Pair output: name one missing context item, one excessive permission and an actor/action/output requirement with a visible pass/fail result.
  • If needed: suggest a receiver that flags a missing unit and links the original without guessing; collect proposals before revealing the debrief.

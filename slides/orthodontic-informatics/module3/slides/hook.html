  • Capture the patient history
  • Prepare treatment consent forms
  • Select, orient and import photos and scans

0–7 · Useful delegation

  • Patient 18 (P-18): fictional teaching patient; invite suggestions, then reveal one possible delegation task per advance.
  • Treatment presentation: organize the diagnosis, plan and options into an explanation for the patient; clinician checks the content.
  • History capture: record concerns and medical/dental responses for verification—not invent answers where history is missing.
  • Consent forms: draft treatment-specific forms; the clinician still explains risks and alternatives and obtains consent.
  • Photos and scans: select the right records, correct orientation and import copies while preserving originals and patient identity.
  • Apps and aligner records: open the correct case and upload only the required records with permitted access.
  • Delegation boundary: these are possible tasks, not deployed automation; the clinician decides and approves. What information would each need?

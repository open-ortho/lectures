7–15 · Who is adapting?

Tell this in the presenter's first person: I used to copy an email into an AI chat, then copy the response back into my email client. Now my agents use connected tools to read email, compose replies and save drafts. This is the presenter's supplied experience; do not embellish it with a sending demonstration, measured time savings or a claim that errors disappeared. The point is who moves the data, not how clever the generated text sounds. Ask the room what work the direct tools remove from this particular interaction. Copying is replaced by permitted operations in the email system; the response is saved where it can be reviewed. Nothing in this account establishes that an equivalent clinical integration exists.

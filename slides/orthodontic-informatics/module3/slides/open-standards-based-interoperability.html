Imaging is part of the case—not the whole clinical record.

27–39 · Choose an exchange contract

  • DICOM: Digital Imaging and Communications in Medicine defines image information and imaging services—not just image pixels or a universal clinical-record container.
  • HL7: Health Level Seven names a standards organization and its healthcare-exchange standards; FHIR is one of those standards.
  • FHIR: Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources organizes exchange into record types, such as Patient, Encounter (visit), Observation (measurement) and DocumentReference (document link).
  • Different parts together: P-18's image exchange and wider history/visit context can need DICOM and applicable HL7 standards in the same workflow.
  • Implementation limits: naming a standard does not establish which records or actions a product supports; imaging is only part of the case.

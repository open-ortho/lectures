Imaging is part of the case—not the whole clinical record.

DICOM is the imaging example. It defines more than image pixels, including related information and services, but is not a universal container for every clinical fact. HL7 is a standards-development organization and a family of healthcare exchange standards; FHIR is one of those standards. FHIR uses modular resources for such concepts as patients, encounters and observations. It does not imply that any product has implemented all of them or all operations. An imaging workflow and a clinical-record workflow may use DICOM and HL7 standards together. Do not teach this as a competition or an inevitable migration from older to newer labels.

Use this as a short retrieval check, not a repeat of clinic infrastructure. Ask who supplies records to a disconnected AI chat. Usually someone selects, copies, labels and explains them. That person has crossed the boundary on behalf of the systems, often without noticing how much clinical context they supplied. A database or image archive is not the same thing as an interface another authorized product can use. Today's practical judgment is whether the next authorized tool can reuse the record without a clinician rebuilding its meaning. The comparison is not human intelligence versus machine intelligence; it is repeated manual adaptation versus software-supported reuse. Transition to a real nonclinical story that makes this distinction concrete.

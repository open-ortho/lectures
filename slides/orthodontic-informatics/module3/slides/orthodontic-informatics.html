Ask for two or three answers before offering examples: a treatment trajectory, unresolved questions, or a list of missing records. Case preparation is our illustration, not a claim that this is everyone's biggest burden. Keep residents' answers in mind and connect them to the information a tool would need. The useful outcome is a reviewable preparation aid, not autonomous treatment planning. We will start with that ambition, uncover the human preparation hidden behind an impressive AI answer, and then ask what software must expose so the next tool does not need the same manual assembly. M1 established why sharing matters; M2 followed the systems. Today we connect them. No live account or patient data is needed.
